## Hi there 👋

Healthcare has never had more data. Or more fragmentation. Clinical, claims, operational and social data all live in different places, move at different speeds and, often, tell different versions of the truth. And in that gap, organizations lose time, confidence and outcomes because teams can’t scale action until they can trust the data behind every decision.

This mission isn’t accomplished by adding another tool to the stack, but rather by becoming the intelligent foundation that ties healthcare together. At its core is CareIntelligence: Our flagship software that aggregates discrete patient data from across every care setting into a single, high-quality record. The result? Stakeholders finally acting on the whole picture, not just a piece of it.

Clinical data surfaces high-risk members before a crisis occurs. Claims history reveals where care gaps have gone unaddressed and where avoidable costs are compounding. Financial data empowers payers and providers to align on sustainable, value-based decisions. And social determinant data ensures the barriers (that no EHR captures alone) aren’t discovered when it’s too late. They’re built right into the care plan.

This isn’t just more data. It’s the right data that’s ready to fuel smarter decisions today, tomorrow and at every touch point in between.
